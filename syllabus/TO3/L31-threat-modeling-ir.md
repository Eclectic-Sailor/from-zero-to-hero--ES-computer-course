# L31: Threat Modeling and Incident Response Basics (EO7)
**Objectives:** Perform a lightweight threat model (STRIDE-lite), define mitigations, outline an IR playbook.

## Resources
- STRIDE primer (OWASP Threat Modeling cheat sheet): https://cheatsheetseries.owasp.org/cheatsheets/Threat_Modeling_Cheat_Sheet.html
- Incident response lifecycle (NIST 800-61r2): https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/SpecialPublications/NIST.SP.800-61r2.pdf

## Tasks
- Apply STRIDE-lite to your service: list assets, entry points, trust boundaries; identify 2–3 threats with mitigations.
- Draft minimal IR checklist: detect, contain, eradicate, recover; roles and communications.

## Example to Analyze
- For a login endpoint, what are spoofing/tampering threats and mitigations?

## Knowledge Check
- What is a trust boundary?
- Why practice containment before eradication?
- What logs/alerts would you rely on?

## Exit Criteria
- One-page threat model + IR plan tailored to your project.
- You can explain at least one mitigation per STRIDE category you listed.
